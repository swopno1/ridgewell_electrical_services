# Calendar View

The **Calendar** module gives you a visual, month-by-month overview of timesheet entries and leave requests — making it easy to spot scheduling gaps, review attendance patterns, and plan ahead.

---

## 1. Accessing the Calendar

Navigate to **Calendar** in the left-hand sidebar. The calendar opens to the **current month** by default. Today's date is highlighted with a blue circle.

---

## 2. Reading the Calendar Grid

The grid displays a full month, with weeks starting on Monday. Each day cell can hold multiple entries.

### Colour Coding

Every entry pill is colour-coded by type and status so you can read the grid at a glance:

| Colour | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 Green | **Approved Timesheet** — hours confirmed by a manager |
| 🟡 Amber | **Pending Timesheet** — submitted, awaiting manager review |
| 🔴 Red | **Rejected Timesheet** — returned for correction |
| 🔵 Blue | **Annual Leave** |
| 🟣 Violet | **Sick Leave** |
| ⚪ Grey | **Unpaid Leave** |

A legend below the calendar grid is always visible for quick reference.

### Entry Count Badge

A small number in the top-right corner of each day cell shows the total entries for that day. If more than three entries exist, a **+N more** label appears at the bottom of the cell — no entries are ever hidden, they are accessible via the Day Detail Panel.

---

## 3. Viewing Day Details

Click on any day cell that contains entries to open the **Day Detail Panel** directly below the calendar grid.

The panel shows every entry for that day, including:

- **Employee name** (visible to Admins and Managers)
- **Status badge** — Approved, Pending, or Rejected
- **Time range** — e.g. 07:00 – 15:30
- **Project name** for timesheet entries
- **Total hours** logged
- **Leave type, status, and full date range** for leave requests

Clicking a timesheet row opens that timesheet's detail page. Clicking a leave row navigates to the Leave module.

To close the panel, click the **✕** button in the panel header, or click the same day cell again.

---

## 4. Navigating Months

The controls in the top-right of the calendar let you move between months:

| Control | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **← (Previous)** | Go back one month |
| **→ (Next)** | Go forward one month |
| **Today** | Jump to the current month |

---

## 5. Role-Based Views

What the calendar shows depends on your account role:

| Feature | EMPLOYEE | MANAGER | ADMIN |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Your own timesheets** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Your own leave requests** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **All employees' timesheets** | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **All employees' leave requests** | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Employee names on entry pills** | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |

> **Note for Admins & Managers**
> On busy days with many employees, the cell shows a condensed summary. Click the day to open the Detail Panel and see the complete list — every entry is always accessible, no matter how many exist.

---

## 6. Tips for Effective Use

- **Spot clashes early**: Admin and Manager views make it easy to see overlapping leave and shifts across the team on a single day.
- **Jump to details quickly**: Click any entry in the Day Detail Panel to go straight to the full timesheet or leave record.
- **Check pending work**: Amber (pending) entries on the calendar are a useful visual reminder of timesheets still awaiting approval.
