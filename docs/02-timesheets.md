# Timesheet Logging

The **Timesheets** module is where you log your daily labour hours and record your work scope. This data drives project tracking, payroll processing, and performance auditing.

---

## 1. Entering Your Daily Hours

You should log your hours at the end of each shift to ensure accuracy.

1. **Access the Form**: Navigate to **Timesheets** in the sidebar.
2. **Select a Date**: Choose the date you are logging for.
3. **Enter Time Parameters**:
   - **Time On**: The time you started work.
   - **Time Off**: The time you finished work.
   - **Break Duration**: Total unpaid break time in minutes (e.g. 30 or 60).
4. **Assign to Project**: Select the correct **Client / Project** from the dropdown.
5. **Scope of Work**: Add a brief, specific description of your tasks (e.g. "Installed new conduits in Section B; tested emergency lighting").
6. **Submit**: Click **Submit Timesheet**.

> **Automatic Calculations**
> The system calculates your **Total Hours** (Time Off − Time On − Break) and any **Overtime** if you exceed the standard daily threshold (typically 8.0 hours).

> **One Entry Per Day**
> You can only submit one timesheet per calendar day. If you need to correct an entry, edit the existing one while it is still in **Pending** status.

---

## 2. Managing Your History

Your previous entries appear in the **Timesheet History** table below the entry form.

### Editing Entries

- Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon on any **PENDING** entry.
- Update the necessary fields and click **Update Timesheet**.

### Deleting Entries

- Click the **Delete** (bin) icon on a **PENDING** entry.
- Confirm the deletion in the dialogue.

> **Locked Entries**
> Once a timesheet has been **Approved** or **Rejected**, it is locked. Contact your manager if a correction is needed on a locked entry.

---

## 3. Status Indicators

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🟡 **PENDING** | Submitted and awaiting manager review |
| 🟢 **APPROVED** | Verified by a manager — ready for payroll |
| 🔴 **REJECTED** | Returned for correction — check the Feedback column for your manager's notes |

---

## 4. Viewing on the Calendar

All approved, pending, and rejected timesheets are visible on the **Calendar** page. Each day cell is colour-coded by status so you can see your working history at a glance. Click any day to see the full detail for that date.

See the [Calendar View](../calendar) documentation for more information.

---

## 5. Best Practices

- **Be specific**: Avoid vague notes like "Worked on site." Use precise descriptions.
- **Log daily**: End-of-week catch-up increases the chance of errors in time estimation.
- **Check the project**: Billing time to the wrong job creates administrative rework.
